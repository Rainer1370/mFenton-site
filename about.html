Attorney & Founder

Committed to Your
Family's Future

Michele Fenton is a New York family law attorney dedicated to providing clear, strategic, and compassionate representation to individuals and families navigating some of life's most difficult moments. Based in Huntington, she serves clients throughout Nassau and Suffolk Counties and the greater Long Island area.

Michele understands that family law cases are rarely just about legal outcomes — they are about real people, real children, and real futures. She approaches every case with rigorous preparation and genuine care for the people she represents.

Whether you are facing a contested divorce, a custody dispute, or need guidance drafting a prenuptial agreement, Michele is committed to protecting what matters most to you.

"My clients are going through some of the hardest moments of their lives. I take that seriously — every single time."

— Michele Fenton

Growing With Our Clients

What's Coming to Michele Fenton PLLC

As our firm grows, so does our commitment to the communities we serve. We are developing new programs to support families beyond the courtroom.

Support Groups

Facilitated peer support for individuals navigating divorce and co-parenting — a space to share, heal, and move forward.

Law Internships

Opportunities for pre-law and law students to gain hands-on experience in family law under Michele's direct mentorship.

Mentoring Program

Connecting young women in Nassau and Suffolk Counties with legal professionals to explore careers in law and advocacy.

Community Workshops

Free public seminars on family law rights, child support, and navigating the court system for Long Island residents.

Pro Bono Clinic

Limited pro bono consultations for qualifying low-income families facing urgent family law matters on Long Island.

Legal Resource Library

A curated online library of guides, templates, and explainers for families navigating New York family law matters.
