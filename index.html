Comprehensive Family Law Services

Every family situation is unique. Michele Fenton PLLC provides thoughtful, experienced legal guidance through life's most challenging transitions — with a focus on results and your long-term wellbeing.

Divorce & Separation

Contested and uncontested divorce, legal separation, and negotiated settlements that protect your financial future.

Child Custody & Parenting

Drafting and reviewing prenuptial and postnuptial agreements that protect both parties with clarity and fairness.
