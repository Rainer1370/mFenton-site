What We Do

Practice Areas

Michele Fenton PLLC provides experienced representation across a full range of family law matters. Every case is handled with the dedication, strategy, and compassion you deserve.

Divorce & Separation

Whether contested or uncontested, divorce is one of the most significant legal events in a person's life. Michele guides clients through every stage — from initial filings through negotiated settlements or litigation — with the goal of protecting your financial and personal interests. Legal separation is also available for those who are not ready for formal divorce.

Child Custody & Parenting Time

Custody disputes require careful, strategic advocacy. Michele works to develop parenting plans that serve the best interests of your children while protecting your rights as a parent. This includes initial custody determinations, enforcement of existing orders, and modifications when circumstances change.

Equitable Distribution

New York is an equitable distribution state, meaning marital assets are divided fairly — not necessarily equally. Michele has experience navigating the division of real estate, retirement accounts, business interests, investment portfolios, and other marital property to ensure you receive a fair outcome.

Child Support & Spousal Maintenance

Support arrangements must reflect your family's actual needs and financial circumstances. Michele handles initial support determinations and post-divorce modification proceedings for both child support and spousal maintenance (alimony), ensuring orders are fair and enforceable.

Orders of Protection

If you or your children are in a situation involving domestic violence or harassment, an order of protection can provide critical legal safeguards. Michele assists clients in obtaining emergency and final orders of protection in Family Court and Supreme Court proceedings, and also represents clients who need to respond to such orders.

Prenuptial & Postnuptial Agreements

A well-drafted prenuptial or postnuptial agreement protects both parties and provides clarity before life circumstances change. Michele drafts and reviews marital agreements that are thorough, equitable, and enforceable under New York law.

Paternity & Parentage

Establishing legal parentage is often a necessary foundation for custody and support proceedings. Michele assists clients in paternity proceedings, acknowledging parentage, and other matters relating to the legal recognition of parent-child relationships.

Not sure if we handle your specific matter? Contact us — we're happy to discuss your situation.
